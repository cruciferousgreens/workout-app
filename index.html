    Progression defaults

    How the app suggests your next session.

    Progression modeRPE-based adds reps inside the range first, then weight, when the top set hits the RPE trigger. Linear adds the increment every session with no RPE needed. %1RM sets each session's load from a percent of the training max (or best estimated 1RM); scheduled deload weeks scale that load back by the deload intensity.

    RPE trigger thresholdWhen your top set comes in at or under this RPE, the app suggests progressing next session.

    Increment type & valueHow much load to add on a progression: a fixed amount (lb or kg) or a percent of the current weight.

    Default focusThe rep-range preset applied to new programs and exercises.

    Default rep rangeMin and max reps for double progression: add reps inside the range first, then add weight and return to the bottom of the range.

    Time stepFor timed exercises, how many extra seconds to hold before adding weight.

    Vary rep ranges by weekOptional undulating periodization for new programs: each program week can inherit its own rep range.
